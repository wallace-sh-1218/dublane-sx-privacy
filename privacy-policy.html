# Privacy Policy / 隐私政策 **Dublane** — macOS audio & video dubbing editor **Publisher / 发布者:** Dublane SX **Contact / 联系方式:** **Effective date / 生效日期:** November 21, 2026 **Last updated / 最近更新:** November 21, 2026 --- ## English ### 1. Who we are Dublane ("the app", "Dublane") is a local audio and video dubbing editor for macOS, published by Dublane SX ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what the app does with your information, what stays on your Mac, and the limited cases in which the app uses the network. This policy applies to the Dublane macOS application distributed through the Mac App Store and to its in-app purchase. It does not apply to any third-party website or service you may reach through a link. ### 2. Summary - We do not have user accounts and we do not ask for your name, email address or phone number to use the app. - We do not include advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs. - Your videos, audio, projects, transcripts, translations, voice recordings and generated speech stay on your Mac. We do not upload them to us or to any third party. - The only network activity is downloading optional AI models, communicating with Apple's StoreKit for purchases, and opening links you explicitly choose. - We do not sell, rent or share personal data. We do not use your content to train models. ### 3. Information we do not collect We do not collect, receive, store or transmit: - personal identifiers such as your name, email address, phone number or Apple ID; - your media files, project files, transcripts, translations or generated audio; - voice recordings or microphone audio; - device identifiers, advertising identifiers or precise location; - usage statistics, feature analytics, crash reports or diagnostics; - contacts, calendars, photos, health data or any other sensitive data. The app has no server that receives your content. Dublane's App Store privacy label is **Data Not Collected**. ### 4. Information processed on your device The following information is created and stored only in the app's sandbox container on your Mac, under your control: - **Media and projects.** Files you import, edits, segment metadata, thumbnails, transcripts, translations and generated audio. They remain on your Mac until you delete the project or the app. - **Microphone audio.** If you record a voice-over or a reference voice, audio is captured only while you are actively recording and is written to a local file. The app never records in the background and never uploads recordings. - **Settings and preferences.** Language, output folder, theme, model URLs, purchase-unlock cache and similar preferences, stored locally. - **File access bookmarks.** macOS security-scoped bookmarks that let the app reopen files you selected after a restart. They are stored locally and are not transmitted anywhere. - **Local service state.** The app may run a translation helper that listens only on the loopback interface (`127.0.0.1`) on your Mac. It is not reachable from the network. AI processing — speech recognition, translation, text-to-speech and vocal separation — runs locally using model files that are either bundled with the app or downloaded at your request. Your content is not sent to a server for inference. ### 5. Network access The app uses the network only in the following cases. **5.1 Optional model downloads.** When you choose to download an AI model (for example speech-recognition, translation or text-to-speech weights), the app requests the file from the URL shown in the app's model list. Depending on your configuration this is `huggingface.co`, the mirror `hf-mirror.com`, or an address you entered yourself. As with any download, the hosting provider may receive your IP address, request time, user agent and the requested file name. We do not control those providers and do not receive a copy of your request. Downloaded files are stored locally in the app's container. **5.2 In-app purchase.** The optional Pro unlock is a non-consumable in-app purchase processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple provides the app only with the entitlement status (whether the purchase is active). We do not receive your payment card, billing address or Apple ID. Apple's handling of purchase data is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). **5.3 Links you open.** Privacy-policy, support, Gemma-terms and open-source license links open in your default browser. Those pages are operated by their respective owners and are subject to their own policies. The app does not contain remote ads, remote configuration, A/B testing, update pings or any other background network calls. ### 6. Third parties We do not share personal data with third parties, because we do not collect any. The following parties may be involved only as described above: - **Apple** — App Store distribution, StoreKit payments and, if you opt in, system-level crash reports handled by macOS. See [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). - **Model hosting providers** — Hugging Face (`huggingface.co`) or the mirror you configure (`hf-mirror.com`), solely to serve the model file you requested. - **Google Gemma** — the optional translation model is a Gemma model. Its download and use are governed by the [Gemma Terms of Use](https://ai.google.dev/gemma/terms) and the [Gemma Prohibited Use Policy](https://ai.google.dev/gemma/prohibited_use_policy), which you accept in the app before the first download. We do not sell or rent data, and we do not disclose data for advertising or cross-context behavioral advertising. ### 7. Legal bases (EEA/UK users) Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we process the limited data described above on the following bases: performance of the service you requested (local processing and model downloads), your consent (opting in to download a model or accept the Gemma terms), and our legitimate interest in operating a secure, functional app. Because we do not collect personal data on our servers, most data-subject requests will concern data that exists only on your own device. ### 8. Your rights and choices - **Access, correction, deletion.** Your content and settings live on your Mac. You can inspect, edit or delete them in the app, or remove the app's container to erase everything. - **Microphone.** You can deny or revoke microphone access at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone. Recording features will be unavailable without it. - **Model downloads.** You can decline any download and still use the app's editing features that do not depend on that model. - **Purchases.** Manage or request refunds for in-app purchases through your Apple account. Deleting the app does not cancel or refund a purchase. - **Privacy questions.** Contact us at . Because we hold no personal data about you, we may be unable to locate any record to export or erase; we will still respond to your request within the time required by applicable law. If you are in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland or another jurisdiction with a data protection authority, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. ### 9. Data retention We retain no personal data on our systems. Data on your Mac is retained until you delete it. Uninstalling the app and removing its container deletes the app's local data; files you exported elsewhere remain wherever you saved them. ### 10. Security The app runs in the macOS App Sandbox and requests only the permissions it needs (microphone, user-selected files, outbound network). Model downloads use HTTPS where the provider supports it. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but because your content never leaves your Mac through us, the primary protection is the security of your own device and Apple account. ### 11. Children The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us, contact us and we will address it. ### 12. International users The app itself does not transfer your content internationally. Model downloads may be served by providers located outside your country, and those requests are subject to the providers' own policies. Apple processes purchases in accordance with its own terms. ### 13. Changes to this policy We may update this policy when the app's features or legal requirements change. The current version is always available at this page, and the "Last updated" date above will change. Material changes will be reflected in the App Store listing where required. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy. ### 14.
